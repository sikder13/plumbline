# Open Sign inspiration: local businesses

Studied on 19 October 2026: 10 sites, all honoured on Awwwards (Honorable Mention), from 2024 to 2026, chosen to cover restaurants and cafes, barbers and salons, trades and home services, auto, and fitness. Each award and date was read from its Awwwards entry; each site was opened in a headless browser at 1440 and 390 wide, scrolled, and its loaded faces and headings read. Screenshots stayed in a scratch folder, not in the repository.

This adds to `reports/nz-tourism-1/wave-3/INSPIRATION.md` (20 travel and wine sites), which still applies. Borrow patterns and philosophy only, never assets, code or layouts.

Where the sources were thin: the Framer Gallery and Siteinspire render their lists with scripts and returned nothing readable to a plain fetch; godly.website now redirects to another site. Awwwards was the one source that could be read and dated, so every reference below is from it.

## The ten

### 1. Sunbeam Bagels & Coffee (cafe, Bryan, Texas)
Awwwards Honorable Mention, 23 July 2026. sunbeambagels.com
- **Hero:** a dim photo of their own room, and three words stacked large in a soft serif: "Coffee. Breakfast. Lunch." One outlined button, "Menu/Order".
- **Hours and place:** in the header itself, both sides of the wordmark: "Come On In: 7am - 3pm" and "Start Your Day Right in Bryan, Tx". The first thing read after the name is when the door is open.
- **Menu:** each item is named by its ingredients as a heading, full stops between: "Bagel. Sausage. Cheese. Egg." The list reads like a sign board, not a table.
- **Signature:** a polaroid pile of their own photos that scatters as you scroll, and a small "Listen for Mmms" sound switch, off by default.
- **Type:** a soft display serif (Belgard) with a monospace (Forma DJR Mono) for small labels and times.

### 2. Burrito Madre (restaurant, Belgrade)
Awwwards Honorable Mention, 9 July 2026. burritomadre.rs
- **Hero:** one product photograph, and the product's name in a heavy condensed face set huge, the letters passing in front of and behind the burrito.
- **Hours and place:** a "Locations" page and a city switch in the footer; not on the home page.
- **Menu:** one item per screen band, name large, a short line under it.
- **Signature:** the closing wordmark, the full name across the width in the brand orange on a deep green ground.
- **Type:** Deacon, condensed and loud, with Figtree for text.

### 3. Little Amps Coffee (cafe and roaster)
Awwwards Honorable Mention, 20 December 2025. littleampscoffee.com
- **Hero:** a slow film of their own shop behind a custom wordmark. (A discount pop up covers it on arrival: the thing to avoid.)
- **Hours and place:** in the footer, by shop.
- **Menu:** shop cards for beans; the cafe menu is a separate page.
- **Signature:** the brand's own drawn mascots and a running line, "Tasty Coffee and Chill Vibes."
- **Type:** two monospaces (Necto Mono, Roboto Mono) for every label, a custom face for the name.

### 4. Bernice Bakery (bakery)
Awwwards Honorable Mention, 28 February 2025. bernicebakery.com
- **Hero:** an overhead spread of their own cakes edge to edge, a heavy black grotesk headline over it ("A Baking Love Affair"), and one round pink badge.
- **Hours and place:** pick up rules stated plainly where you order: from 11 am to 5 pm, no pick ups on Sunday or Monday.
- **Menu:** cookies and cakes as product tiles; one cake photographed inside an arch with "Celebrate with cake" set around the arch.
- **Signature:** the round "Order" button that sits on the edge of the arched photo.
- **Type:** Balto (heavy grotesk) with Apfel Grotezk.

### 5. Salon Heleen Hülsmann (salon and showroom, Amsterdam)
Awwwards Honorable Mention, 17 July 2025. salonheleenhulsmann.nl
- **Hero:** a full bleed street photograph of their own people, no headline at all on desktop; on a phone the name is stacked in a tall condensed face over the photo.
- **Hours and place:** in "Get in touch", a quiet page.
- **Menu:** "What we do" as short paragraphs, no prices.
- **Signature:** restraint. Thin underlined links in one row at the top, and a strip of their own social posts.
- **Type:** Didot for display, Aktiv Grotesk for everything else.

### 6. Rendezvous Barbers (barbershops, Toronto)
Awwwards Honorable Mention, 31 July 2024. myrendezvous.ca
- **Hero:** a flat field of one orange, and one sentence in a large serif that turns italic halfway: "Toronto-based barbershops *specializing in the latest modern short hair styles*". A small film of a cut beneath it.
- **Hours and place:** a "Locations" block with the count and the hours as plain text: "Monday to Friday 10:00 to 20:00".
- **Menu:** services as a short list under each location.
- **Signature:** three numbered steps around a phone showing their booking app.
- **Type:** GT Super (serif, roman and italic in one line) with GT Alpina Typewriter for small labels.

### 7. Haven Constructions (builder, Melbourne)
Awwwards Honorable Mention, 10 June 2026. havenconstructions.com.au
- **Hero:** the name alone, "Haven", in one blue, as wide as the screen. Two tiny captions in the corners under it: "EST. 1990" and "MELBOURNE". The photo comes only after.
- **Hours and place:** the town is in the hero caption; phone and email are in the menu.
- **Menu:** projects as large photographs with one line each.
- **Signature:** the photo below the wordmark opening wider as you scroll.
- **Type:** Suisse, one family, the hero at display weight and everything else small.

### 8. Cedar Springs Landscapes (landscaping, Oakville, Ontario)
Awwwards Honorable Mention, 20 November 2025. cedarsprings.net
- **Hero:** a full bleed photograph of one finished backyard; a lowercase, light headline low on the left ("get away every day"); a vertical "scroll" label; a blurred slice of the next photo at the right edge.
- **Hours and place:** the towns served are the H1 for search ("Landscaping in Oakville, Burlington & Ancaster"), kept visually quiet.
- **Menu:** process steps, each with one photograph.
- **Signature:** "Start Your Backyard Resort" as the one action, repeated at the close over another full bleed yard.
- **Type:** Akzidenz Grotesk Next, light and lowercase, with ITC Blair for tiny caps.

### 9. Classics Garage (auto, concept)
Awwwards Honorable Mention, 3 May 2024. A three.js concept, not a working garage, kept for its one idea.
- **Hero:** a script wordmark over radiating lines that feel like speed or a tunnel.
- **Hours and place:** none (a concept).
- **Menu:** none.
- **Signature:** a garage scene with plain switches: lights, colours, auto rotate. Every switch changes something real in the scene.
- **Type:** Oswald for the labels, DM Sans for text.

### 10. Core Atelier Pilates (pilates studio)
Awwwards Honorable Mention, 25 March 2026. coreatelierpilates.com
- **Hero:** a warm photograph of their own room, full bleed, and a serif line with one italic word: "elevated pilates, crafted *for you.*" One small pill button.
- **Hours and place:** a contact block; classes on a schedule page.
- **Menu:** classes as named cards (group, private).
- **Signature:** the closing wordmark, lowercase, so large it is cropped by the bottom of the screen.
- **Type:** Minion Pro (roman and italic) with Albert Sans.

## Patterns worth borrowing for small local businesses

| ID | Pattern | From | Suits |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Hours in the header, before anything else | Sunbeam | any shop with fixed hours |
| L2 | Items named by their parts, full stops between | Sunbeam | menus, service lists |
| L3 | Name or product set huge, interleaved with the photo | Burrito Madre | one strong product photo |
| L4 | Overhead spread of the work as the hero ground | Bernice | bakeries, tattoo flash, floors |
| L5 | One round action on the edge of a framed photo | Bernice | one primary action |
| L6 | One sentence, roman turning italic | Rendezvous, Core Atelier | a business with one clear line |
| L7 | Hours as one plain line per day group, tabular | Rendezvous | every business |
| L8 | Wordmark as wide as the screen, tiny corner captions (year, town) | Haven | builders, trades, a short name |
| L9 | Lowercase light headline low on a full bleed yard | Cedar Springs | landscaping, outdoor trades |
| L10 | Switches that each change one real thing | Classics Garage | anything with a few states (days, services) |
| L11 | Giant cropped wordmark to close | Burrito Madre, Core Atelier | any brand with a short name |
| L12 | Monospace for times and small labels | Sunbeam, Little Amps, Rendezvous | hours, phone numbers |

## What these sites avoid, and what to avoid from them

- None of the strong ones open on a slideshow; each opens on one photograph or one word.
- None hide the town: it is in the header, the hero caption or the H1.
- Avoid: Little Amps' discount pop up over the first screen; Cedar Springs' and Burrito Madre's cookie banners covering half a phone screen; Classics Garage's loading spinner before anything shows.
